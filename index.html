About

The unglamorous parts are the product

Most business software succeeds or fails on details nobody demos: a balanced journal entry, a leave balance that respects the joining date, a payroll run that handles a mid-month shift change.

I work across the whole stack — designing the schema, encoding the rules in the backend, and building the screens that make those rules understandable. My focus is on systems that are correct first and pleasant to use second, because an accounting or payroll tool has no room for “almost right”.

When I am not building, I am usually reading about systems design, trading tools or whatever new framework is making noise that week.

Backend

C#, ASP.NET Core 8, EF Core, REST APIs, ASP.NET Core Identity, SQL Server, SQLite

Frontend
